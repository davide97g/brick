# Suno pack — "The Walk" (v1, instrumental)

In Suno, use Custom mode with **Instrumental** switched on. Aim for a take near 1:30. If it runs longer,
the edit trims at a bar boundary and keeps the real ending, so a long take is fine. Download **stems** if
they're offered: the drums stem makes the footstep kicks and the cuts exact. Drop the mp3 (and stems) in
`video/song/`.

## Style

```
instrumental minimal deep house, 112 BPM, A minor, felt piano motif of four notes that returns throughout, warm round kick like footsteps, soft clock-tick percussion, glassy bell ping accents, dry rimshot, deep sub bass, long airy pads, spacious and sparse, patient build with thickening hats, one full-stop break of silence, full groove after the break, ends on the piano motif alone, clean ending
```

## Exclude

```
vocals, vocal chops, spoken word, choir, rap, big EDM drop, dubstep riser, trap hi-hats, guitar, orchestral, lo-fi, vinyl crackle
```

## Structure

If your Suno version still shows the lyrics box with Instrumental on (or you keep Instrumental off and
paste only tags), use this. Otherwise the style string carries the arc on its own.

```
[Intro: piano motif alone, clock ticks]
[Build: pads and sub enter]
[Groove: kick enters, sparse]
[Break: one bar of silence]
[Build: hats thicken, faster]
[Drop: full groove, kick like footsteps]
[Outro: piano motif alone]
[End]
```

What the edit needs from the arc, in order:

- **~0:00–0:09:** the piano motif alone. This is Act 0, the phone waking by itself.
- **~0:09–0:20:** the kick arrives. Act 1, the tap.
- **A clear stop somewhere around 0:30–0:40:** Act 2, "Not yet.".
- **~0:40–1:00:** a build that gets busier. Act 3, the fast cuts of the world outside.
- **~1:00:** a proper landing. Act 4, the gate passes and the walk back starts.
- **The last ~10 s:** the motif alone again. The end card.

## Picking a take

- The motif matters most. A take where the piano line is memorable beats a take with a better drop.
- Reject takes where Suno sneaks in vocal chops or a big EDM riser.
- Timings can be ±8 bars off the list above; the cut follows the song, not the other way round.
- If the take isn't at 112 BPM, keep it anyway when it feels right: every cut follows the tracked beat
  grid, not the tempo in the prompt.
